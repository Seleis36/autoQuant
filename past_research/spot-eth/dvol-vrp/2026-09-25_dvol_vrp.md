# dvol-vrp — ETH implied volatility and the variance risk premium as a spot timing signal

**Date:** 2026-09-25
**Asset class:** spot-eth (Binance ETHUSDT daily closes at 00:00 UTC; Deribit ETH DVOL, 2021-05 → 2026-09-24)
**Short name:** `dvol-vrp`
**Status:** **Failed**

## TL;DR

Two competing hypotheses were tested. (A) **Risk premium**: when 30-day implied vol (Deribit DVOL) sits well above realised vol, the market is paying for insurance, and holders of spot ETH are compensated with higher forward returns (the Bollerslev–Tauchen–Zhou 2009 VRP-predicts-returns result). (B) **Vol shock**: rising IV marks risk-off selling. On 5.4 years of data:

- **ICs are weak.** VRP z vs 7-day forward: **0.051 IS / 0.103 OOS**. IV level z vs 30-day: 0.18 IS / 0.02 OOS. IV shocks have no reliable sign. Quintile sorts are non-monotone IS.
- **Standalone gates are no better than vol-scaled buy-and-hold.** Median of 48: 0.29 full / 0.27 OOS, vs 0.39 / 0.44 for vol-scaled B&H.
- **Overlays on the trend filter.** The 96-cell grid's best "VRP high × TF" cells lift both halves (IS 0.25 → 0.35–0.43, OOS 1.20 → 1.31–1.47). But the **a-priori** versions do not: `1{IV > RV}` *hurts* (0.73 → 0.40 full), `1{VRP > 180d median}` gives 0.17 IS / 1.33 OOS, and the continuous tilt gives 0.33 / 1.37 with alpha t **0.34 / 0.94**. A control gate on realised vol alone (`RV < 180d median`) produces a similar OOS lift (1.53) and a worse IS. The OOS improvement is mostly "avoid high-RV regimes", which the trend filter's vol scalar already does.

## Data

- `data/DERIBIT_DVOL_ETH_1d.parquet`, `data/DERIBIT_DVOL_ETH_1h.parquet`: Deribit public `get_volatility_index_data`, from 2021-03-24. Fetcher: `code/spot-eth/dvol-vrp/fetch_dvol.py`. Bars are stamped at the open; the daily Close is the value at D+1 00:00 UTC.
- Realised vol: trailing 720 hourly log returns (30 days) from Binance ETHUSDT, annualised, taken at the same D+1 00:00 instant.
- Weight set at the D+1 00:00 close, applied to day D+1's return. 10 bp per side. Sample 2021-05 → 2026-09 (DVOL warm-up). IS 2021-05 → 2023-12 (2.7 y), OOS 2024-01 → 2026-09 (2.7 y).
- The trend filter's own Sharpe on this window is only 0.25 IS / 1.20 OOS (2021-05 → 2022 was its worst stretch). Every comparison below is on the same window.

IV exceeds 30-day RV on 68% of days, so a positive premium is the normal state and not informative by itself.

## Lookahead check

DVOL and RV are both read at the D+1 00:00 instant the trend filter rebalances. `backtest` applies weights to the next day. All z-scores and medians are trailing.

## Verdict

**Failed.** The binding constraint is **signal strength and sample length**. The VRP carries a 7-day IC of ~0.05–0.10, and its overlay benefit is not distinguishable from a plain realised-vol filter on 2.7 + 2.7 years of data. Revisit only when DVOL history is long enough to include a second bear market out of sample.

**Variants tried:** 24 IC cells, 96 gate cells, and 10 a-priori overlays.
